Make checkout location work worldwide. Zone locations take optional delivery `areas` (towns, districts or estates such as Westlands or Lekki), matched against the address's `city` without case, accents or extra spaces; at the same zone order an area match now beats a postcode match, which beats a region and then a country-wide match. `cart/shipping-location`, `cart/shipping-address` and `cart/contact` accept an optional `area`, stored as the city and validated server-side, and `cart/shipping-regions` also returns the countries the zones cover (`shipsTo`) and their `areas`. A postcode entry followed by a space (`SW1 `) matches that outward code only. Local pickup is offered from every zone that covers the address. Hosted checkout shows the country as a dropdown by name, offers the postcode only where zones use it and never requires it, adds an Area dropdown with "Somewhere else", carries standard autofill tokens, and shows zero-decimal prices such as JPY correctly. Switching the cart's currency drops a shipping method priced in the old one. Admin → Shipping gets an Areas field per country and a read-only "Check an address" tool (`admin/shipping/check`, admin only). Region names now cover Nigeria, Canada, Australia, India, Brazil, South Africa, the UAE and Japan as well as Kenya and the US. Existing zones, carts and orders keep working unchanged.
